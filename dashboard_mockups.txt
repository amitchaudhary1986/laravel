**Section 1: Engagement Metrics**

*   **Metric:** Daily Active Users (DAU)
    *   **Visualization:** Line chart with time on X-axis (Day/Week/Month toggle) and count on Y-axis.
    *   **Textual Representation:**
        ```
        DAU
        ^
        |  /\
        | /  \
        |/    \
        +------------------> Time (Day/Week/Month)
        ```

*   **Metric:** Monthly Active Users (MAU)
